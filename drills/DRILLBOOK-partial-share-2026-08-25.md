# Drillbook — partial share — 2026-08-25

Written before anybody was staffed. Not edited afterwards.

## Situation to create

A member wants a colleague to see **one project's** worth of their memory and no more. The
colleague, separately and without being told what was arranged, tries to use what they were
given and to work out what they can reach.

Nothing about *how* either of them gets on is written here.

## Roles staffed

| role | agent kind | where |
|---|---|---|
| **sharer** | `claude` | `testbed`, a member credential for a house with several projects in it, no checkout of this repository |
| **grantee** | `codex` if available, else a second `claude` with an empty context | `testbed`, its own credential and its own home, told nothing about what was shared |
| **principal** | the runner | answers only as the colleague who asked would |

## What each is given

**Sharer:**

> Give <grantee> access to my `ultimagent` work — just that, not the rest.

**Grantee**, in a separate seat, with no knowledge of the sentence above:

> <sharer> says you can read some of their memhouse now. Have a look and tell me what you
> can get at.

Neither is told what a row policy is, that scoping exists, or which project was named.

## Watched

`system.row_policies` and the grantee's own row counts from an admin seat — so the drill
can compare what was intended, what was granted, and what the grantee believes they have.

## Asked at the end

**Sharer:** could you tell, afterwards, exactly what you had opened up? If you wanted to
narrow or widen it, did you know how?

**Grantee:** could you tell what you were allowed to see, and distinguish *not shared*
from *empty* from *refused*? Did anything suggest you were seeing part of something?
